# Frequently Asked Questions (Q&A)

## My titles or settings disappeared.

Clearing browser cache or site storage can reset local data. If you exported a backup previously, you can restore your data from the "Others" tab.

## I cannot apply titles to Twitch.

Verify that your token is valid, has the `channel:manage:broadcast` scope checked, and that the game category name is exact. Click "Sync" to fetch official Twitch category names.

## Title updated on Twitch, but OBS's native Stream Info dock didn't update.

Refresh OBS's native Stream Info dock or press `Ctrl + R` while focused on that dock.

## Can I use a custom sound folder?

Yes. Select your folder via "Select Sound Folder". Due to web browser security policies, you need to re-select the folder after restarting OBS or reloading the dock.

## Settings in my standard browser are not showing up in the OBS Dock.

Local storage is isolated between your standard web browser and the OBS Browser Dock. Export a backup from one and restore it in the other to sync settings.
